## Objective
Implement the core fee math in fees.py as compute(invoice, payments, policy) for invoices with no payments — accruing outstanding principal x monthly_rate_pct/100/30 on every calendar day from due_date + grace_days + 1 through as_of inclusive in exact Decimal arithmetic, flooring the summed accrual to the cent and capping it at floor_to_cent(amount x cap_pct/100) — so that a 100.00 invoice with policy grace_days 5, monthly_rate_pct 3.0, cap_pct 25.0, as_of 2026-02-15 yields fee 3.10 and owed 103.10, as_of 2026-01-15 yields fee 0, as_of 2026-01-16 yields one day's accrual, an exact accrual sum of 2.999 floors to 2.99, and an above-cap accrual yields exactly the cap.
